Programs · learn · gather · support

The work, in three movements.

Civic education gives the community a voice. Community engagement keeps culture moving. Humanitarian assistance moves care where it's needed. One organization, holding all three at once.

01

Civic education

Learn the system.
Find your voice.

KOA teaches how public systems work and prepares advocates to participate with confidence — so the community doesn't just live inside the system, it shapes it.

  • Civic education and exploratory learning
  • Advocacy 101 training
  • Advocacy trips to Washington, D.C.
  • Local advocacy activities
  • Annual Youth Leadership Conference
02

Community engagement

Keep culture
moving.

Community visits and workshops create space to learn, gather, and pass knowledge forward. Connection in motion: arts, sports, and cultural events that strengthen relationships across ages and places.

  • Community visits and workshops
  • Arts and cultural events
  • Sports and youth connection
  • Intergenerational learning
03

Humanitarian assistance

Move care where
it is needed.

KOA supports food, water, shelter, education, and vocational training for displaced families. Care with purpose: relief addresses immediate needs while education and training open longer-term possibility.

  • Food, clean water, and shelter
  • Education support
  • Vocational training
  • Assistance for displaced families
