# Planning Session — 2026-08-09 (v4)

**Participants:** Oliver P, Perplexity AI
**Topic:** Community dictionary, audio training, translation services, court interpretation, community contributions

## Summary

Oliver confirmed that the community must be deeply involved in this website because it is a nonprofit for the community. He specified:

1. **Community-moderated dictionary:** Definitions scraped from community resources + community-uploaded. Moderated by the community. Best definitions for words that are not yet defined. Multiple translations, synonyms, antonyms.
2. **Community audio uploads:** For training speech-to-text and text-to-speech models for Karen language. Goal: a Karen LLM that can hear and understand spoken Karen.
3. **Translation/interpretation services:** Approved translators and interpreters listed in a directory. KOA can offer translation and interpretation services.
4. **Court interpretation partnership:** Courts have a shortage of Karen interpreters. Public defenders cannot find translation services. KOA can fill this gap.
5. **Community contribution features:** Feature requests (what features/services they want), service suggestions (what the org should provide), collaboration requests, donations.
6. **Community-driven admin:** People who are approved translators/interpreters can have elevated roles.

## Research Findings

- Living Dictionaries platform: collaborative, multimedia, editable dictionaries for under-resourced languages.
- Mozilla Common Voice: community audio collection for speech training.
- Court interpreter shortage: documented nationwide, especially for less-common languages.
- Karen interpreter shortage specifically mentioned in community discussions.
- Google Speech-to-Text requires minimum 100 hours of audio for custom model training.
- Community-moderated dictionaries (Urban Dictionary model) need human review to maintain quality.
