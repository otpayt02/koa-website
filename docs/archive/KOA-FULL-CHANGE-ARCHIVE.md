## Major architectural evolution and purpose

- Static ``public/koa`` HTML/CSS/JS landing surface: preserves the original multi-page public content and asset inventory as a locally inspectable baseline.
- Cinematic landing components such as ``CinematicLanding``, ``KOALogoIntro``, ``KAGlyphField``, ``KarenGlyphField``, ``SealAssembly``, ``LoomWeave``, ``SunshineRays``, and ``PartnerMarquee``: turn the landing page into a staged, scroll-linked narrative while retaining semantic content.
- Canonical localized App Router route ``app/[lang]/page.tsx``: consolidates the runtime around one React/vinext application and four locale surfaces.
- Content/data modules and provenance-aware studios: separate content, translation retention, dictionary review, design preview, and admin-only authoring concerns from public rendering.
- Motion foundation and normalized scroll: make cinematic effects deterministic, responsive, and suppressible under reduced-motion preferences.
- PowerShell runner/stopper: make the exact local version reproducible, prevent duplicate vinext processes, and avoid accidental deployment.
- Tests and verification scripts: protect locale, seal, glyph, motion, rendered HTML, admin access, and runner contracts.
