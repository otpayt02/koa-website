# Home cinematic ideas

These are reviewable possibilities, not implemented or approved behavior.

## Explore after Phase 7 proof

- Compare the current chapter holds with a slower reading study across all four locales, changing pacing only when evidence identifies a specific readability problem.
- Add culturally reviewed audio or performance cues only after ownership, consent, transcript, captions, and a complete silent alternative are documented.
- Replace draft photographic references with organization-approved originals carrying provenance, subject consent, reuse scope, and removal contacts.
- Evaluate richer partner storytelling only after the relationship record and logo permission are approved; never infer partnership from a public URL.
- Test a reusable frame-diff report that links each canonical frame to sanitized desktop, mobile, and Motion-off proof.

## Explicitly deferred

- Donation processing and tax-receipt claims.
- Automatic or background translation approval.
- A second public static product alongside the React application.
- Decorative motion that does not clarify the story or an interaction state.

## Review gate

Move an idea into the design/spec only after an accountable reviewer records the decision, source, privacy/rights boundary, acceptance criteria, and required proof.
