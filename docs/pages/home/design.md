# Home cinematic design

## Purpose

The home route introduces Karen Organization of America through a slow identity-first film: the exact supplied seal arrives, K and A gather around it, the seal migrates, the delayed O completes the mark, and five readable community chapters lead to reviewed participation paths.

## Visual system

- Use Hermes navy as the continuous field, paper for readable copy, Karen red for interaction, and restrained gold for ceremony.
- Keep the supplied seal geometry and embedded English/Karen circumference lettering exact. Rotate only the aligned annular crop; the inner seal remains stationary.
- Keep the first-party glyph field sparse, persistent, seeded, and behind measured foreground geometry.
- Treat each chapter as one message per viewport with a protected reading hold.
- Use the partner handoff only for approved relationship records; unverified organizations stay in admin preview.

## Responsive composition

Desktop uses the authored `1800vh` runway. Mobile uses `1440vh`, preserves the same chronological story, and favors readable compositions over miniature desktop framing. Motion off and reduced motion reveal the complete semantic story without RAF-driven canvas motion, glimmer, marquee motion, or delayed access to content.

## Interaction and accessibility

Native scroll controls story progress. Hover and keyboard focus receive the same single-pass glimmer where motion is enabled. Canvas layers remain decorative; headings, copy, navigation, partner names, and involvement actions remain semantic and keyboard reachable.

## Approval boundary

This design is local-only. Translation publication, partner claims, photography reuse, donation processing, deployment, and official publication require recorded review and separate approval.
