We're building the most complete home the S'gaw Karen people in America have ever had — and the most ambitious thing built for a Karen language. A national movement, one brick at a time. This page is the wall before the paint dries.

Meet S'gaw-Mango Join the build

01 · The centerpiece

S'gaw-Mango.
The Karen AI, on its way.

A ~15-billion-parameter model built to read, speak, and teach S'gaw Karen — side by side with English, Thai, and Burmese, the languages Karen families already live in. It won't be finished by a company. It'll be finished by a community.

S'gaw-Mango · 15B
Bilingual fork · text + vision · English ↔ Karen · ไทย · မြန်မာ

Teaches

Every part of speech

Breaks sentences down — grammar rules, common phrases, translations — the way a patient teacher would, in the language itself.

Looks up

Dictionary, live

Every word traced to real instances — crawled from the internet, from texts and databases — so meanings come from how people actually use them.

Sees

Vision & OCR

A vision model trained on Karen script — reading documents, signs, and pages so the written language enters the machine age.

02 · The build, brick by brick

What's coming.

Everything below is either already under construction or queued behind it. Statuses are honest: live, in build, or coming.

01

The National Registry

A verified directory of Karen churches (with addresses and pastors), businesses and restaurants, and community resources across the entire United States. One lookup, the whole diaspora on the map.

In build
02

Events & Tournaments

Sepak takraw, volleyball, and soccer — listed, counted, and celebrated. Plus the cooking table: submit your recipe, the community upvotes, and the top dishes get featured on the front thumbnail.

Coming
03

The Music Showcase

Karen music front and center — starting with thra eh keh lah, and the Karen artists across the internet, linked to where their music already lives.

Live now
04

The Podcast

The KOA podcast — stories, language, and the movement, in episodes you can carry with you. Episodes land here the day they publish.

Coming
05

History & News Hub

The story of who the Karen people are — told fully, with the receipts — alongside the news as it happens. The memory and the front page, in one place.

In build
06

Community Verification

A username system and verification tiers — from contributor to verified word-auditor to verified admin — so the people who carry the language carry the keys too. Your voice, your pictures, your translations, your word requests, all submitted from your account.

Coming
