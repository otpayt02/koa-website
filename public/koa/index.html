KOA at a glance

A national organization built through community leadership.

KOA connects founding history, public advocacy, cultural life, and practical support.

01 · Founded

2018

Omaha, Nebraska KOA was formally established after a national gathering.

Its purpose is rooted in collaboration and unity.

02 · Founding reach

Twenty states

More than fifty leaders took part in the founding gathering.

Communities joined around a shared national voice.

03 · Programs

Three areas

Civic education builds knowledge and public leadership.

Community engagement and humanitarian assistance extend the work.

04 · Community

All generations

Youth, families, and elders take part in cultural and public life.

Language, arts, sports, and gathering strengthen belonging.

05 · Coalition

Eleven organizations

Across the United States community organizations shaped KOA’s founding network.

Each brings local knowledge to national connection.

06 · Connection

One public channel

KOA on Facebook shares photographs, gatherings, and program updates.
