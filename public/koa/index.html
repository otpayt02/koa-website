Who we are

Built by Karen communities across the United States.

KOA was officially founded on August 8, 2018, in Omaha, Nebraska. It united two long-standing organizations around a shared commitment to Karen communities.

01

Vision

To unite and empower Karen community thriving in the U.S. and advocating for justice in Burma.

02

Mission

Advocate for rights and well-being, strengthen unity, promote educational success, and stand in solidarity with Karen people in Burma.

KOA raises awareness and speaks out against persecution and human rights violations while supporting collaboration among Karen communities in the United States.

History of KOA

June 13, 2018

KOUSA and KAO leaders discussed working collaboratively and strengthening unity.

August 8, 2018

More than fifty leaders from twenty states met in Omaha, Nebraska.

One national body

The organizations merged to form the Karen Organization of America.

What we do

Three ways we serve.

Choose a program to see what KOA does and who it supports.

Leadership and public voice

Understand public systems and advocate with confidence.

  • Civic education and exploratory learning
  • Advocacy 101 training
  • Advocacy trips to Washington, D.C.
  • Local advocacy activities
  • Annual Youth Leadership Conference

Culture and connection

Create places for community knowledge and relationships to grow.

  • Co-host annual Karen Martyrs' Day with local Karen communities
  • Host an annual sports tournament
  • Host arts and cultural contests
  • Annual community visits and workshops

Practical solidarity

Mobilize support for displaced Karen children, youth, and families.

  • Fundraising for food, water, shelter, and other basic needs
  • Support for displaced Karen people along the Burma-Thailand border
  • Annual Kawthoolei trips
  • Education and training for displaced Karen children and youth
