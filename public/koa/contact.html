Contribute

Bring the thing
you've been holding.

The movement runs on what the community sends in. Write it down, send it over, and it goes on the list — in the order the community decides.

  • Word requests — the never-translated-but-necessary, first
  • Translations — documents and texts that need to cross over
  • Recipes — for the cooking table; the community upvotes
  • Music — an artist or song that belongs on the wall
  • Church & business listings — for the national registry
  • Voices & pictures — for the Karen AI, with your name in it

Before you write

A clear first message
goes farther.

01

Introduce yourself

Describe the goal
