About · founded August 8, 2018 · Omaha, Nebraska

Fifty leaders. Twenty states. One organization.

The Karen Organization of America was formed when the leaders of the Karen diaspora decided that one voice, raised together, carries further than twenty raised alone.

The founding

From conversation
to one national body.

  1. June 13, 2018

    The conversation

    Leaders of KOUSA and KAO sit down to discuss working collaboratively and strengthening unity across the diaspora.

  2. August 8, 2018

    The national gathering

    More than fifty leaders from twenty states meet in Omaha, Nebraska.

  3. 2018

    KOA is formed

    The organizations join to form the Karen Organization of America — one national body for the Karen community in the United States.

  4. Now

    The work, and what's coming

    Civic, community, and humanitarian programs across the U.S. — and a next chapter the community is building with us. See what's coming →

Vision & mission

Purpose you can act on.

Vision

Thriving Karen communities

United and empowered in the United States — advocating for justice in Burma.

Mission

Rights and well-being

Advocate. Educate. Strengthen unity. Stand in solidarity. KOA strengthens rights, well-being, educational success, and relationships between Karen communities.

What endures

Identity lives when shared

Culture, language, relationships, and public presence keep community knowledge moving — built for continuity, connecting historical purpose to the work communities need now.
