01 — Who we are

United by
purpose.

KOA was officially founded on August 8, 2018, in Omaha, Nebraska.

More than fifty leaders from twenty states came together to form one national body.

Vision

Thriving Karen communities in the U.S., advocating for justice in Burma.

Mission

We advocate for rights and well-being, strengthen unity, promote educational success, and stand in solidarity with Karen people in Burma.
