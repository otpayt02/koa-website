Who we are

United by
purpose.

KOA was officially founded on August 8, 2018, in Omaha, Nebraska. More than fifty leaders from twenty states came together to form one national body.

Vision

Thriving Karen communities in the U.S., advocating for justice in Burma.

Mission

Advocate. Unite. Educate. Stand in solidarity.

We strengthen rights and well-being, educational success, and connection between Karen communities.
