A National Home

Uniting Karen communities across twenty states, building belonging in the land we now call home.

From the Pacific to the Atlantic, Karen families have planted roots in American soil while carrying the spirit of Kawthoolei in their hearts.

၂

One National Body

Born from unity. Forged by fifty leaders. Established for all Karen people.

On August 8, 2018, more than fifty Karen community leaders from twenty U.S. states met in Omaha, Nebraska. Two organizations became one national body.

၃

Public Leadership

Advocating for Karen voices in the halls of power, from local councils to the Capitol.

Our youth and leaders travel to Washington, D.C., meeting with legislators, sharing our stories, and ensuring that Karen perspectives shape the policies that affect our communities.

About KOA

Vision

To unite and empower Karen community thriving in the U.S. and advocating for justice in Burma.

Mission

The Karen Organization of America advocates for the rights and well-being of Karen communities in the United States and abroad.

History

Founded August 8, 2018 in Omaha, Nebraska, when fifty leaders from twenty states united as one national body.
