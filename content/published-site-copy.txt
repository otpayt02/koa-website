S'gaw-Mango AI · ကညီကျိာ်တၢ်ပညိၣ်
H1: A Karen language tool, built by the Karen community.
A Karen language tool, built by the Karen community. Dictionary, translation, glyph recognition, and sentence construction — every word reviewed, every voice belonging to someone.
Contribute a wordSearch the dictionary
How it works
H2: From syllables on paper to syllables in the air.
S'gaw-Mango AI connects community-reviewed language tools into one pipeline. Every capability traces to a real evidence artifact and a real reviewer.
01
H3: Dictionary search
Community-reviewed Karen–English dictionary with provenance on every entry. Search headwords, browse by topic, or suggest a correction.
Available nowSearch the dictionary
02
H3: Translation with review
English to S'gaw Karen translation backed by the community dictionary. Every result shows its source: dictionary, community-approved, or model draft.
Declared — dictionary-backed with human reviewRequest translation
03
H3: Glyph recognition (OCR)
Computer vision model trained to read S'gaw Karen glyphs and syllables from printed and handwritten sources.
Measured — 88.7% mAP on held-out Roboflow test setSubmit a sample
04
H3: Sentence construction
Bilingual sentence builder that helps Karen speakers and learners construct grammatically correct sentences with guided translation.
In development — community review pipeline activeHelp build sentences
05
H3: Speech understanding
Record a spoken Karen sentence for community transcription. Every recording becomes training data only after human review and speaker consent.
In development — speech-to-text not yet trainedRecord a sentence
06
H3: Karen music generation
Chord-chart editor and Karen-style music sheet generator. Preserve existing hymns and compose new music in Karen styles.
In development — editor works; generative music not yetSee the music editor
Data provenance
H2: Every word is reviewed. Every voice belongs to someone.
S'gaw-Mango AI does not learn from scraped text or unreviewed submissions. Every training datum traces to one approved translation proposal whose reviewer is a real human contributor.
Every word reviewed by a human before it becomes training data.
Unreviewed S'gaw Karen is not training data — it is a proposal.
Community contributors own their corrections; the language stays with the community.
No public claim about capability is made without measured evidence.
Join the review pipeline
Community contribution
H2: The language grows because people give to it.
Every correction, recording, and sentence pair helps the next generation of Karen speakers. Contributions pass through the Language Studio before they become training data.
01
H3: Share a word or phrase
Submit a Karen word, phrase, or correction. It enters the Language Studio review queue where a human reviewer checks accuracy and provenance.
Submit a word
02
H3: Record your voice
Record a Karen sentence for community transcription. Your voice becomes training data only after review and your recorded consent.
Record a sentence
Start contributingLearn about the Language Studio
Truth in claims
H2: What we say, and how we know it.
KOA does not overclaim. Every capability statement carries a truth-state label so visitors know what is measured, what is in development, and what is planned.
H3: Declared
The capability exists and is backed by a specific implementation, but has not been independently measured on a held-out test set.
H3: Measured
The capability has been evaluated on a specific test set with a published metric. The measurement date and conditions are recorded.
H3: In development
Work has begun but the capability is not yet available for public use. The spec describes the planned approach.
H3: Planned
A design exists but no implementation has started. Community input is welcome at this stage.

Karen Music Director · တၢ်မၤစၢၤကညီဂီတ
H1: The sound of home, carried forward.
Preserve Karen hymns, equip music directors with a bilingual editor, and connect the Karen music tradition to the language stack. The sound of home, carried forward.
Try the editorSend a song
Five chapters
H2: A story told in song.
၁
H3: The sound of home
Karen hymns carry the memory of villages, refugee camps, and church gatherings across three continents. The music director program preserves these songs in durable digital form.
၂
H3: What we preserve
Handwritten chord charts, church hymnals, oral arrangements, and community recordings — each one catalogued, rights-checked, and archived with provenance.
၃
H3: What we build
A bilingual chord-chart editor with Karen typing mode, print-ready PDF export, and a chord-chart library of 21+ Karen music pieces. Built for Karen music directors in America.
ၔ
H3: Who leads the music
Karen music directors lead worship, teach young singers, and arrange hymns for community gatherings. KOA equips them with tools and connects them to each other.
၅
H3: How to contribute
Upload a handwritten chart, submit a recording, or suggest a lyric correction. Every contribution passes through a rights review before publication.
The editor
H2: A bilingual tool for Karen music directors.
The chord-chart editor supports Karen typing mode, chord placement above lyric lines, and print-ready PDF export. Use it to preserve existing hymns or compose new arrangements.

H3: Bilingual editor
Write lyrics in S'gaw Karen and English side by side. Karen typing mode maps your keyboard to Myanmar/Karen script.
Implemented — Karen + English, reviewed

H3: Print-ready sheet music

Implemented — PDF export with Karen script
03
H3: Chord-chart library
21+ Karen music chart files including hymns, youth songs, and contemporary pieces. Hand-scanned charts from community elders.
Declared — library grows with each contribution

H3: Karen music generation
Melody templates using Karen-scale grammar. Generative music in Karen styles is planned but requires dataset provenance first.
In development — melody templates only; no generative model yet

Preview only. The editor requires the local Karen Music Director service running on port 4177. If the frame above is blank, start the service or visit the editor directly. This embed uses option 4 (iframe demo) from the integration spec; production will use a subdomain with reverse proxy.
Karen music style
H2: Meter, melody, and meaning.
Karen hymns follow established patterns — predominantly 4/4 meter, verse–chorus form, I–IV–V–I harmony with stepwise melody within an octave. Lyrics use S'gaw Karen script with English chord symbols above the line.
Predominantly 4/4 meter; some 3/4 waltz hymns

H1: ကညီကျိာ်တၢ်ပညိၣ်လၢပှၤတဝၢတဖၣ်တ့ထီၣ်။
တၢ်ပညိၣ်ကညီကျိာ်လၢပှၤတဝၢတဖၣ်တ့ထီၣ်။ တၢ်လံာ်ခီယ့ၣ်၊ တၢ်ပိၤဘၣ်၊ ဒီးတၢ်မၤတၢ်စၢၤကညီကျိာ်အဂီၢ်။

How it works
H2: From syllables on paper to syllables in the air.
S'gaw-Mango AI connects community-reviewed language tools into one pipeline. Every capability traces to a real evidence artifact and a real reviewer.
01
H3: Dictionary search
Community-reviewed Karen–English dictionary with provenance on every entry. Search headwords, browse by topic, or suggest a correction.
Available nowSearch the dictionary
02
H3: Translation with review
English to S'gaw Karen translation backed by the community dictionary. Every result shows its source: dictionary, community-approved, or model draft.
Declared — dictionary-backed with human reviewRequest translation
03
H3: Glyph recognition (OCR)
Computer vision model trained to read S'gaw Karen glyphs and syllables from printed and handwritten sources.
Measured — 88.7% mAP on held-out Roboflow test setSubmit a sample
04
H3: Sentence construction
Bilingual sentence builder that helps Karen speakers and learners construct grammatically correct sentences with guided translation.
In development — community review pipeline activeHelp build sentences
05
H3: Speech understanding
Record a spoken Karen sentence for community transcription. Every recording becomes training data only after human review and speaker consent.
In development — speech-to-text not yet trainedRecord a sentence
06
H3: Karen music generation
Chord-chart editor and Karen-style music sheet generator. Preserve existing hymns and compose new music in Karen styles.
In development — editor works; generative music not yetSee the music editor
Data provenance
H2: Every word is reviewed. Every voice belongs to someone.
S'gaw-Mango AI does not learn from scraped text or unreviewed submissions. Every training datum traces to one approved translation proposal whose reviewer is a real human contributor.
Every word reviewed by a human before it becomes training data.
Unreviewed S'gaw Karen is not training data — it is a proposal.
Community contributors own their corrections; the language stays with the community.
No public claim about capability is made without measured evidence.
Join the review pipeline
Community contribution
H2: The language grows because people give to it.
Every correction, recording, and sentence pair helps the next generation of Karen speakers. Contributions pass through the Language Studio before they become training data.
01
H3: Share a word or phrase
Submit a Karen word, phrase, or correction. It enters the Language Studio review queue where a human reviewer checks accuracy and provenance.
Submit a word
02
H3: Record your voice
Record a Karen sentence for community transcription. Your voice becomes training data only after review and your recorded consent.
Record a sentence
Start contributingLearn about the Language Studio
Truth in claims
H2: What we say, and how we know it.
KOA does not overclaim. Every capability statement carries a truth-state label so visitors know what is measured, what is in development, and what is planned.
H3: Declared
The capability exists and is backed by a specific implementation, but has not been independently measured on a held-out test set.
H3: Measured
The capability has been evaluated on a specific test set with a published metric. The measurement date and conditions are recorded.
H3: In development
Work has begun but the capability is not yet available for public use. The spec describes the planned approach.
H3: Planned
A design exists but no implementation has started. Community input is welcome at this stage.

Karen music style
H2: Meter, melody, and meaning.
Karen hymns follow established patterns — predominantly 4/4 meter, verse–chorus form, I–IV–V–I harmony with stepwise melody within an octave. Lyrics use S'gaw Karen script with English chord symbols above the line.

Five chapters
H2: A story told in song.
၁
H3: The sound of home
Karen hymns carry the memory of villages, refugee camps, and church gatherings across three continents. The music director program preserves these songs in durable digital form.
၂
H3: What we preserve
Handwritten chord charts, church hymnals, oral arrangements, and community recordings — each one catalogued, rights-checked, and archived with provenance.
၃
H3: What we build
A bilingual chord-chart editor with Karen typing mode, print-ready PDF export, and a chord-chart library of 21+ Karen music pieces. Built for Karen music directors in America.
ၔ
H3: Who leads the music
Karen music directors lead worship, teach young singers, and arrange hymns for community gatherings. KOA equips them with tools and connects them to each other.
၅
H3: How to contribute
Upload a handwritten chart, submit a recording, or suggest a lyric correction. Every contribution passes through a rights review before publication.
The editor

The chord-chart editor supports Karen typing mode, chord placement above lyric lines, and print-ready PDF export. Use it to preserve existing hymns or compose new arrangements.

Karen music style
H2: Meter, melody, and meaning.
Karen hymns follow established patterns — predominantly 4/4 meter, verse–chorus form, I–IV–V–I harmony with stepwise melody within an octave. Lyrics use S'gaw Karen script with English chord symbols above the line.
Predominantly 4/4 meter; some 3/4 waltz hymns

H3: Grammar rules & examples
