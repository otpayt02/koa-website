Do not turn the event's participant count into an evergreen organization claim.

**National Youth Advocacy Program reel.** In the [July 20 reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1745744503354021), KOAmerica says Saw Ta Nay Tha from California discussed empowering young leaders to advocate for their communities. **Capitol-stop reel.** A second July 20 feed item says “Current stop: The U.S. Capitol.” Its full caption and direct reel link were not verified. Keep it grouped with the Washington trip as an unplaced media candidate.

## Search and answer design

| Destination | Suggested title and heading | Answer-focused content | Source/structured-data notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | “Karen Organization of America” | State who KOAmerica serves and link to the dated Updates stream | Use `Organization` data only for verified name, URL, logo, and contact fields |
| About | “About the Karen Organization of America” | Answer “When was KOAmerica established?” with “The Facebook profile says August 2018,” until the founding record is approved | Link internally to History; do not add a full postal address from “1407 Hazel Ave” |
| Advocacy | “Karen community advocacy and civic education” | Explain the work, then cite the Washington trip and NYAP reel with dates | `Article` or `VideoObject` data belongs to the specific update, with a verified publication date and thumbnail |
| People | “KOAmerica people and leadership” | Give current roles only after confirmation; link to dated announcements | No unsupported staff permanence or institutional endorsement |
| Updates | “KOAmerica news and community updates” | Lead each item with who, what, when, where, and what the visitor can do now | Canonical URLs, visible dates, source links, and `NewsArticle` only when the page is an actual editorial article |

Write visible answers in ordinary page text so search engines and AI answer systems can attribute them. Use “Karen Organization of America” near the start of titles and headings to distinguish the organization from unrelated KOA entities. Keep location claims tied to confirmed city/state or event venues; avoid inventing a service area from follower locations. Publish reviewed language versions with correct `lang` and `hreflang`; translated copy and image alternatives need human review. AEO and GEO labels do not justify unsupported claims or stuffed alt text.

## Editorial sync queue

For each new Facebook item, record its permalink, publication date, original caption, media IDs, owner, category, current/archive state, website destination, proposed headline, factual checks, image alt, image caption, and approval. Publish a dated Updates item first; change evergreen Home, Programs, People, or Advocacy copy only when the new item changes a confirmed standing fact. Recheck expiring calls to action. Preserve Facebook links as provenance and keep a correction path if the original post changes or is removed.
