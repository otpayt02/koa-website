## Users

Working record, inferred on 2026-09-30 from the supplied KOA brief, repository documentation, and the current public site because the structured-question tool was unavailable: S'gaw Karen people and families in the United States are the primary audience. Community members seeking language continuity, connection, programs, and a shared organizational home take priority when visitor needs conflict. Volunteers, partners, and supporters are secondary visitors.

## Product Purpose

KOA is the public home for the Karen Organization of America: a cinematic, multilingual-aware mission experience that connects people to its story, programs, community voices, music, future language work, and ways to make contact or register interest. Success is a clear, trustworthy route into community connection without claiming unfinished work is already available.

## Positioning

KOA presents a community-rooted national home for S'gaw Karen people in America, combining cultural continuity, practical community pathways, and forthcoming language technology. The community's participation and review are treated as source material, not decorative proof.

## Operating Context

The canonical implementation is a React/Vinext App Router application with public localized routes, language pivots, a cinematic landing experience, and a protected administration area. The public site offers navigation to About, Programs, Stories, Music, Coming Soon, Contact, and an early-access interest form. The static `public/koa` surface is a reference, not a second product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Maintain the K–seal–A choreography: the supplied authentic seal is the sole O; K and A are individual glyph fields, not solid letters or exposed guides.
- Preserve native scrolling, complete mobile/reduced-motion behavior, keyboard access, readable foreground content, and pointer-transparent decorative canvas.
- Keep translations, community relationships, photographs, cultural copy, donation behavior, public claims, and publication review-gated.
- The current phase is local-only; deployment and public release are not implied by code, browser, or build evidence.
- The public site contains future-facing items (including language-AI work); their readiness, operational scope, and claims require owner/community confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

The site is cinematic, dark, and restrained, using Hermes blue fields, red interaction, and gold ceremony. It preserves the supplied seal without reconstructed circular wording, extra type arcs, duplicate hero scenes, tall ellipse halos, hard rays, visible K/A outlines, solid faces, or construction traces. Motion is purposeful and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Product Principles

1. Community knowledge precedes presentation and technical building.
2. Show what is ready; label what is future-facing.
3. Preserve cultural identity through supplied, reviewable material rather than fabricated detail.
4. Make the public experience immersive without sacrificing accessible, ordinary navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, focus visibility, adequate contrast, readable content-led layouts, localization-aware text handling, reduced-motion operation, and decorative layers that are hidden from assistive technology and do not intercept input.
