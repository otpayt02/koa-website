# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Karen families and community members in the United States, neighbors and volunteers, youth leaders, congressional and policy audiences, and people seeking ways to support KOA.

## Product Purpose

The Karen Organization of America website explains KOA's mission, programs, cultural preservation work, and federal advocacy, and gives visitors clear paths to get involved, contact KOA, or support the work.

## Positioning

KOA connects practical resettlement support, living Karen culture and language, youth leadership, and policy advocacy in one community-led organization.

## Operating Context

The website is a public, bilingual-ready community and advocacy presence. It uses confirmed copy from the KOA website content draft and preserves unresolved factual details as clearly marked open items.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home is a static, single-viewport landing experience with selectable service pillars and direct navigation shortcuts.
- Public sections include Home, Mission, About, Programs, Advocacy, Donate, and Contact.
- The landing page uses a Karen flag image, KOA seal, and Karen glyph-formed K/A mark.
- No scroll-driven motion is required on the landing page.
- Payment processing, contact delivery, leadership details, historical milestones, addresses, social links, and tax disclosures remain unconfirmed until supplied.

## Brand Commitments

Use the Karen Organization of America name, the KOA seal, Karen glyphs, Karen flag imagery, a deep navy and cream foundation with gold and red accents, and a cinematic but readable editorial tone.

## Product Principles

- Community voice comes before institutional polish.
- Cultural continuity is active, public, and intergenerational.
- Visitors should understand what KOA does and what to do next quickly.
- Unconfirmed facts must never be presented as confirmed facts.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

The site should support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, meaningful image alternatives, and reduced-motion preferences while keeping the landing experience usable without animation.
