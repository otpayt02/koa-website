# KOA Butter / Mid reference language

Use this for KOA visual work when Oliver asks for the Meta, Butter or Mid style. Read `docs/koa-butter-reference-inventory.json` and `docs/koa-butter-design.md` for provenance and the implementation contract. This skill records reference-derived design choices, not permission to publish.

## Transferable decisions

- Compose the desktop Home as two unequal experiences of equal width: living K–official seal–A and bilingual identity on the left, documentary photograph on the right. Stack them on narrow screens. Preserve native scroll and the site's actual multipage routes.
- Give English titles an editorial serif voice, Karen its proper Myanmar-script font, and utility labels a restrained sans. Long organization names move from warm white to gold across the bilingual pair; red belongs only to small seal/glyph accents.
- Make the border mission statement the second focal point. Supporting prose stays below it with a bounded reading measure. Existing donation and language navigation remain usable.
- Preserve the original seal's circular wording. Mask/layer the supplied artwork; never type a replacement inscription from a reference. Keep the halo circular, bounded, soft and secondary.
- Keep K/A silhouettes assembled. Use differently sized glyph sprites and three parallax layers; near layers lean more. Cache glyph paint, sort layers at resize, and use `1-exp(-k*dt)` for frame-rate-independent response. Breathing is tiny, not a formation or scatter sequence.
- A slow Gaussian gold wake highlights existing grid cells, temporarily changes glyphs at distance thresholds/clicks, and decays back to the original grid. No fast cursor dot, hover scramble, inertial replacement tail, Burmese numeral formation or expanded particle pool.
- Use a 15-second three-tab chapter with a fade/blur entrance. Pause cycling during focus/hover and hidden-document states; implement arrow/Home/End keys and roving focus. Honor OS reduced motion and the explicit QA freeze.
- For the brief entrance, use the reference's 0/89/144/233/377/610 ms sequence, with an early headline and accessible actions. Use real exponential response math; the reference's claimed Taylor expansion is incorrect.
- Glass is a transparent chapter surface over the real glyph field, with one quiet hairline. Square gold actions, small press travel and a single easing family keep the nonprofit tone serious.
- Reference HTML includes sample facts, outdated Karen names, captions, donation destinations and beyond-border wording. Reuse design observations only. Current approved content and the photo provenance ledger win.
